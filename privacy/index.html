Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Learn to Love Money ("LTLM," "we," "us") publishes educational content, free tools, and a quiz at this site. This page explains what information we collect when you use it, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

Most of this site works without you giving us anything. A few features do involve information you choose to provide:

  • Email signups. The newsletter forms on this site (in the header, footer, and blog email-gate) ask for your email address so we can send you new posts and tools.
  • Quiz responses. The Investor Type Quiz is hosted on its own page and may ask for your answers to generate a result. It does not ask for financial account information.

We don't collect payment information, government IDs, or financial account details anywhere on this site.

How we use it

Email addresses submitted anywhere on this site — the newsletter signup, the blog's subscriber-only unlock, and the Investor Type Quiz — are stored in the same private spreadsheet so we can send you content from Learn to Love Money: new posts, tools, and occasional updates. We don't sell your email address or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies & local storage

Some pages use your browser's local storage (not a tracking cookie) to remember small preferences — for example, that you've already unlocked a subscriber-only blog post, so you're not asked again on your next visit. This information stays on your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else.

Third-party links

This site links out to other tools and services we don't control (for example, the Investor Type Quiz, and eventually an email service provider once one is connected). Those destinations have their own privacy practices — this policy only covers pages on learntolovemoney's own site.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the site changes. The date at the top will always reflect the most recent update.
